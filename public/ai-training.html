4. Payment & Mobile Money (MoMo) Troubleshooting

Q: What if I didn't get the MoMo payment prompt on my phone?

A: Sometimes telecom network traffic delays push prompts. You can approve it manually:

  • MTN Mobile Money: Dial *170# → Option 6 (My Wallet) → Option 3 (My Approvals). Enter your MoMo PIN to approve the pending payment.
  • Telecel Cash: Dial *110# → Check Pending Approvals.
  • AirtelTigo Money: Dial *110# → My Account → Approvals.

5. Order Tracking & Delivery Troubleshooting

Q: I paid for data but I haven't received it yet. What should I do?

A: Follow these quick steps:

  1. Go to our Live Order Tracker: https://swiftdatagh.shop/order-status
  2. Enter your Order Reference or phone number to see real-time fulfillment status.

  4. You can also check your data balance directly on your phone:
    • MTN: Dial *138# → Balance Inquiry.
    • Telecel: Dial *126#.
    • AT: Dial *124#.
